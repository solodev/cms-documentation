Integrating **Customer.io** with your forms is a powerful way to streamline customer engagement and automate communication. By connecting your forms to Customer.io, you can seamlessly capture user data and trigger personalized emails, SMS, or other interactions based on their behavior.

## Prerequisites

- Have a [Solodev form](/forms/form-overview/).
- A [Customer.io account](https://fly.customer.io/signup).

## Connect Customer.io to the CMS

You'll need your **Site ID**, **Track API Key**, and **App API Key**, found in Customer.io under Account Settings > API Credentials.

1. Under **Admin**, click **Settings**, then **Config** on the left navigation.

<p><img src="../../../../images/form/settings-main-nav.jpg" alt="Main navigation with Settings link highlighted" style="width: 10%;"></p>

2. Open the **Customer.io** accordion.

<p><img src="../../../../images/form/cio-config-accordion.jpg" alt="Customer.io accordion" style="width: 40%;"></p>

3. Add the Site ID, Track API Key, and App API Key, then click **Submit**.

<p><img src="../../../../images/form/cio-credentials.jpg" alt="Customer.io credentials fields" style="width: 80%;"></p>

## Connect a form (optional)

Only needed if you already have a configured Customer.io custom form -- otherwise a custom form is created on Customer.io automatically on first submit.

1. Open the form and click **Modify**.
2. Open the **Customer.io** accordion.
3. Enter the Customer.io **Form ID**, found on Customer.io under Details.
4. Click **Submit**.
