This feature is helpful if you use an email marketing platform (Mailchimp, Constant Contact, etc.) and need to batch upload a list of emails.

Download every submission in a form as a CSV file. From the form's overview page, open the **...** menu and click **Export**. 

Export downloads the form's entire current set of submissions after you confirm the action. The export does not include options for filtering or selecting specific entries. Same mechanism as [Module Overview's Export](/modules/module-overview/export/).
