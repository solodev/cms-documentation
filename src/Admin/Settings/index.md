The Settings section contains a wide range of functionality for managing your Solodev environment. **Connected Providers** at the top highlights key system connections, such as your SMTP mail provider. 

Click **View all providers** to view and manage all connections under [Providers](/admin/settings/providers/).

<p><img src="../../images/admin/settings/settings.png" alt="Settings dashboard: Connected Providers, and tiles for Collections, Apps, Layouts, Providers, Permissions, Workflow, Reports, and Branding" class="border" style="max-width: 94%;"></p>

**Name** | **Description** 
:--- | ---
[Collections](/admin/settings/collections/) | Generate a category detail page from a page template automatically.
[Apps](/admin/settings/apps/) | Add/manage an app or reorganize your left menu.
[Layouts](/admin/settings/layouts/) | Build and manage reusable Page Layouts and Page Components.
[Providers](/admin/settings/providers/) | Connect the CMS to outside services (Git hosts, SSO, marketing platforms, AI runtimes, and more).
[Permissions](/admin/settings/permissions/) | Manage your users and group permissions.
[Workflow](/admin/settings/workflow/) | Add workflows to your forms.
[Reports](/admin/settings/reports/) | Check your users activity log.
[Branding](/admin/settings/branding/) | Update your branding and update colors.
