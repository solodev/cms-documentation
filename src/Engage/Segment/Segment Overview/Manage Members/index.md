Open a Segment to see **Users in that segment**, and use **Manage Members** to add or remove contacts from it by hand.

<p><img src="../../../../images/engage/manage-members.png" alt="Manage Members form" class="border"></p>

!!! Note
If the Segment is populated from a connected provider (Customer.io), membership is driven by that connection instead &mdash; manual changes here may be overwritten on the next sync.
!!!

**Name** | **Description**
:--- | ---
Name | Start typing to search for a contact by name.
Role | The contact's role within the Segment (for example, View or Admin).
Add | Click the orange **"+"** button to add a new member to your Segment.
Delete |  Click the **"x"** on the right side of the table to remove a member from your Segment.

!!! Note:
A segment member can be assigned one of four roles:

* **View:** Allows a member to see items and content.
* **Stager:** Enables a member to create or modify content but not publish.
* **Editor:** Gives a member the ability to view, create, edit, and publish.
* **Admin:** Provides full editorial control and management of group members.
!!!
