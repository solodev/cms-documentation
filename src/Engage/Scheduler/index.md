# Scheduler

Scheduler swaps a page's content in and out on a predefined schedule, without anyone needing to manually publish the change when the date arrives. Create several versions of a page's content, upload them to a Scheduler, and set the dates and frequency for each to go live.

Scheduler is commonly used to:

- Publish content at a specific date and time.
- Rotate a series of announcements on a page over time.

To create and manage a Scheduler, see [Scheduler](/tutorials/advanced-development/cms/scheduler/) in Tutorials.
