Permanently remove a Scheduler and its scheduled entries. From an open Scheduler, open the **...** menu and click **Delete**.

<p><img src="../../../../images/engage/scheduler-delete.png" alt="Delete confirmation" class="border"></p>

Confirm the item(s) to delete, then click **Submit**. This action cannot be undone.

!!! Note
Deleting a Scheduler does not delete the underlying files &mdash; it removes the grouping and any permissions/display tied to it.
!!!
