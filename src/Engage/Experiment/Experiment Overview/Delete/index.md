Permanently remove an Experiment and its tracked items. From an open Experiment, open the **...** menu and click **Delete**.

<p><img src="../../../../images/engage/experiment-delete.png" alt="Delete confirmation" class="border"></p>

Confirm the item(s) to delete, then click **Submit**. This action cannot be undone.

!!! Note
Deleting a Experiment does not delete the underlying files &mdash; it removes the grouping and any permissions/display tied to it.
!!!
