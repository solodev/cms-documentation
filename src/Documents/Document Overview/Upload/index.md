Add one or more files from your computer into the current folder. From a folder's page, click **Upload**. Optionally you can drag and drop files from your local file system.

<p><img src="../../../images/folders/upload-files.png" alt="Upload Files panel" style="max-width: 40%;" class="border"></p>

**Name** | **Description**
:--- | ---
Drop Area | Drag and drop multiple files from your computer to upload them in bulk.
Browse | Click to browse your computer and select a file to upload.
Upload | Upload and publish the file(s).
Upload + Stage | Upload and stage the file(s).
Upload + Save | Upload and save the file(s).

Each file uploads as soon as it's added and appears in the list below the drop zone with its size and progress.

!!! Note:
A file shown in red already exists in the system and will be overwritten by the new file. Click **Submit** twice to proceed, or click **X** to remove the file.
!!!
