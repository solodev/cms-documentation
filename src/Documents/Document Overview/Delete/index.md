Permanently remove one or more files/folders. From the parent folder's page, select the item(s) with their row checkboxes, then click **Delete** in the toolbar that appears.

<p><img src="../../../images/websites/files/delete-file.png" alt="Delete confirmation for a file" style="width: 45%;" class="border"></p>

**Name** | **Description**
:--- | ---
Submit | Click **Submit** to permanently delete the selected item(s). This can't be undone -- deleting a folder removes everything inside it too.
