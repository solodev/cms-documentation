Documents is a general-purpose file repository, separate from any specific website &mdash; think of it as a shared Dropbox-style space for files for your organizational needs, rather than a website's own web files. It sits alongside Websites, Modules, and Forms in the left navigation.

At the top level, Documents just lists folders.

<p><img src="../images/documents/documents-folder.png" alt="Documents table" class="border"></p>

**Name** | **Description**
:--- | ---
[Add Folder](/documents/add-folder/) | Create a new folder to organize files in.

Click a folder's name to open it and manage its contents &mdash; see [Document Overview](/documents/document-overview/).

!!! Note:
Use the search field at the top to locate a specific folder.
!!!
