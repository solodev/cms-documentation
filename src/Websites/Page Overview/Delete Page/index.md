Permanently remove a page from your website. From an open page, click the **...** menu next to **Publish**, then click **Delete**.

<p><img src="../../../images/websites/pages/delete-page.png" alt="Delete panel for a page" style="width: 45%;" class="border"></p>

**Name** | **Description**
:--- | ---
Name | Confirm which page(s) are checked for deletion.
Submit | Click **Submit** to permanently delete the page. This cannot be undone.

!!!danger Warning:
Deleting a page is permanent. If the page is referenced by a template or included on a page, that reference will break.
!!!
