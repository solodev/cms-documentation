# Stage

Automatically stage all pages and files in a designated folder. From the left tree, select the folder you wish to stage the contents of. Click the **...** menu next to **+ Add**, then click **Stage**. There's no confirmation form to fill out &mdash; this applies immediately.

!!!danger  Warning:
Staging will apply to all pages and files within a selected folder. Make sure you are aware of the folder's contents &mdash; including any files that you wish to remain in draft or publish &mdash; before you click **Stage**.
!!!


<p><img src="../../../images/folders/folder-menu-options.png" alt="Folder actions menu with Stage" class="border"></p>

!!!Note:
On a large folder, staging can take a few moments to finish. Avoid navigating away until it completes.
!!!
