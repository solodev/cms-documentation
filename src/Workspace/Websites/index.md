From the main **Websites** view, you can access the existing websites in your CMS deployment, manage permissions for your website members, and add new sites. 

To modify a website, click on the thumbnail image or the website name in the table. This will open the website’s main dashboard. 

<p><img src="/static/images/websites/websites-empty-table.jpg" alt="Websites screen" style="width: 90%;"></p>

!!!Tip:
Use the search field in the upper right corner to locate or filter specific websites in your CMS.
!!!

**Name** | **Description** 
:--- | ---
Name | Name of the website.
Members | Users with permissions to the specific website.
[Add Website](/workspace/websites/add-website/) | Create a new website in your CMS deployment.

You can access a website by clicking either the image or its name listed in the table.
