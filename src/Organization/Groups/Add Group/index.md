# Add Group

From the [Groups](/organization/groups/) list, click **Add Group**.

<p><img src="../../../images/organization/add-group.png" alt="Add Group form"></p>

**Name** | **Description** 
:--- | ---
Name | The group's name.
Purpose | What the group is for.

Once created, you'll land on the group's [Group Overview](/organization/groups/group-overview/) page, ready to add members.
