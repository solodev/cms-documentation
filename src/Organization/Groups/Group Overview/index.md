# Group Overview

View and manage a specific group. Click a group's name from the [Groups](/organization/groups/) list to open it.

<p><img src="../../../images/organization/group-overview.png" alt="Group Overview with real members"></p>

**Name** | **Description** 
:--- | ---
<a href="/organization/groups/group-overview/modify/">Modify</a> | Update the group's name, purpose, and team image.
<a href="/organization/groups/group-overview/permissions/">Permissions</a> | Review which apps and folders this group has access to.

## Users in group

Every member of this group. Click **Add User(s)** to add more, choosing a role (View, Stager, Editor, or Admin) for each.

## Apps the group has access to

Which apps this group can access. Click **Manage** to grant or change access.

!!! Note:
A group member's role can be one of four levels:

- **View:** Can see items and content. 
- **Stager:** Can create or modify content but not publish.
- **Editor:** Can view, create, edit, and publish.
- **Admin:** Full editorial control and management of group members.
!!!
