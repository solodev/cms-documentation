## Users in group

Every member of this group. Click **View** to see the details of this user.

<p><img src="../../../images/organization/users-in-group.png" alt="Users in group table" class="border" style="max-width: 75%;"></p>

## Apps the group has access to

Which apps this group can access. Click **Manage** to grant or change access. Select what areas of the CMS the group will have access to and assign a role.

<p><img src="../../../../images/organization/group-permissions-admin.png" alt="Apps the group has access to" class="border" style="max-width: 75%;"></p>

!!! Note:
A group member's role can be one of four levels:

- **View:** Can see items and content. 
- **Stager:** Can create or modify content but not publish.
- **Editor:** Can view, create, edit, and publish.
- **Admin:** Full editorial control and management of group members.
!!!
