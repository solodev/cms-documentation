From the [Users](/organization/users/) view, click **Add User**. Adding a new user will send them an email with a link to sign up for an account on your CMS deployment.

<p><img src="../../../../images/organization/add-user-to-group.png" alt="Add User modal" class="border" style="max-width: 40%;"></p>

**Name** | **Description** 
:--- | ---
Name or email | Start typing an email address or name and select it from the list.
Role | The user's role for that group (View, Stager, Editor, or Admin).
Remove | Click the **x** to remove a user.

!!! Note:
A member can be assigned one of four roles:

- **View:** See items and content.
- **Stage:** Create or modify content, but not publish.
- **Editor:** View, create, edit, and publish.
- **Admin:** Full control, including managing other members' permissions.
!!!
