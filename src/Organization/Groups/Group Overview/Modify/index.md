Update a group's details. From [Group Overview](/organization/groups/group-overview/), open the **...** menu and click **Modify**.

<p><img src="../../../../images/organization/group-modify.png" alt="Modify Group form" class="border"></p>

**Name** | **Description** 
:--- | ---
Name | The group's name.
Purpose | What the group is for.
Team Image | Upload an image to represent the group. Recommended size: 200px x 200px.
Advanced | Expand to permanently delete the group.

## Delete

To delete a group, expand the **Advanced** accordion. Type `DELETE` to confirm, then click **Delete**. This cannot be undone.

<p><img src="../../../../images/organization/group-delete.png" alt="Advanced section expanded, showing the group delete confirmation" class="border"></p>
