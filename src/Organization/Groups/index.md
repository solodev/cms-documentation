# Groups

Create groups in your CMS, add members, and manage their roles and permissions. Click a group's name to view or modify its details. Groups are the best way to manage access to all the features of Solodev CMS. Instead of granting permissions on each individual user in your CMS deployment, you can add users to a group and they will automatically inherit the permissions assigned to the group. If you need to remove or change access for a user, simply remove them from the group(s) they are assigned to.
