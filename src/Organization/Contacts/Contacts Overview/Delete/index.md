Permanently remove a contact. From the [Contacts Overview](/organization/contacts/contacts-overview/), open the **...** menu and click **Delete**.

<p><img src="../../../../images/organization/contact-delete.png" alt="Delete contact confirmation" class="border" style="max-width: 40%;"></p>

Review the listed contact, then click **Submit** to confirm. **CAUTION:** This action cannot be undone.
