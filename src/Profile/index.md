# Profile

Manage your account details and security from your profile, reached through your avatar in the lower-left corner of the CMS.

**Name** | **Description** 
:--- | ---
[General](/profile/general/) | Your name, phone, timezone, title, company, session timeout, and profile image.
[Security](/profile/security/) | Your password, Two-Factor Authentication, and API tokens.

!!! Note:
Every field and action on your profile applies the same way regardless of your role -- there's no separate admin-only view. Whether you're an Admin or a View-only user, you see and manage your own account the same way.
!!!
