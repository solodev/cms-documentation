Manage your account details and security from your profile. To open it, click your avatar in the lower-left corner of the CMS.

<p><img src="../images/profile/avatar.png" alt="Avatar in the lower-left corner of the CMS" class="border"></p>

Your profile has two sections: **General** and **Security**.

<p><img src="../images/profile/profile-nav.png" alt="Profile navigation" class="border"></p>

**Name** | **Description**
:--- | ---
[General](/profile/general/) | Update your name, phone, timezone, title, company, session timeout, and profile image.
[Security](/profile/security/) | Manage your password, two-factor authentication, and API tokens.

!!! Note:
Every field and action on your profile applies the same way regardless of your role &mdash; there's no separate admin-only view. Whether you're an Admin or a View-only user, you see and manage your own account the same way.
!!!
