# Manage Profile

Update your account details, including your name, phone number, timezone, title, company, and session timeout.

<p><img src="../../../images/profile/profile.png" alt="Profile form" class="border"></p>

**Name** | **Description**
:--- | ---
First Name | Your first name.
Last Name | Your last name.
Phone | Your phone number. Optional.
Timezone | Your timezone. Used for all dates and times in the CMS.
Title | Your job title. Optional.
Company | Your company name. Optional.
Session Timeout | How long you can be idle before you are signed out. Choose between 20 minutes (default), 40 minutes, or 60 minutes.

## Profile Image

Hover over your profile avatar and click on **Replace Image**.

<p><img src="../../../images/profile/replace-image.png" alt="Replace profile image" class="border"></p>

Then upload a new image or choose one of the provided images.

<p><img src="../../../images/profile/replace-image-modal.png" alt="Replace profile image modal" class="border"></p>

!!!Tip:
This is the image your team members see when collaborating in the CMS. Consider something intuitive, like a headshot or a unique graphic that's easy to identify.
!!!
